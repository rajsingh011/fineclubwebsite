Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee of ₹649, then ₹7 for each order placed through your QR. There is no monthly subscription.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR with the normal phone camera and the menu opens in the browser.

Where does the payment go?

Straight to the UPI ID or QR you set. We never touch your sales money. We only collect the small per-order fee, once a week.

Which printers work?

Any 58 mm or 80 mm roll printer your computer or phone can print to, over USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. Pick your paper size in Settings.

I am not registered for GST. Can I still use it?

Yes. Set GST to 0% and the bill prints as a plain bill. With a GSTIN it becomes a tax invoice with CGST and SGST.

How long does setup take?

Upload your menu from Excel or CSV, print the table QR sheet, and you are live. We help with setup.

What if the internet goes down?

Online ordering needs the internet. Guests can still tell the waiter, and you can bill at the counter once you are back online.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. There is no monthly plan to cancel. Stop using the QR and the fee stops.

Who we are

fineclub ("we", "us") provides software that lets cafes, restaurants and hotels ("cafes") offer a QR menu, online ordering, kitchen screens, bills and rewards. When you order from a cafe through fineclub, the cafe decides what it needs from you, and we process your data on the cafe's behalf to run the service. We also hold some data for our own purposes, such as security, billing the cafes, and preventing misuse.

Why we use it

  • To take your order, show it to the kitchen, and print or send your bill.
  • To tell you when your order is ready.
  • To run rewards, coupons and the leaderboard (which shows nicknames only, never phone numbers or emails).
  • To let cafes see their orders and sales, and to send offers to customers who ordered from them.
  • To keep the service secure and stop fraud, fake orders and cheating in rewards.
  • To bill cafes our fees and keep records the law requires.

We use your data with your consent, to deliver the service you asked for, and where the law requires it.

Who can see your data

  • The cafe you ordered from sees your orders, name and phone number.
  • fineclub staff see data only to run the service, give support and prevent misuse.
  • Service providers that store or deliver data for us: our hosting provider, our database provider, Google (if you use Google sign-in), and WhatsApp or SMS providers if the cafe turns on bill messages.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires us to share.

We never sell your data and we do not show advertisements.

Phone storage and cookies

We keep small items on your phone so the service works: your order keys, your points wallet key, and your sign-in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Order records are kept for the period the cafe needs for accounts and tax, up to 180 days for ordinary orders unless the law requires longer, then deleted.
  • Notification addresses are deleted once the order is served.
  • Points history is kept while your wallet is active.
  • Cafe accounts and billing records are kept as long as the account exists and as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Write to us with the phone number or email you used. We will anonymise your orders and delete your points wallet. Some records (such as tax bills) may need to be kept for the period the law requires. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Staff logins are protected by PINs that are stored in scrambled form and by lock-outs after wrong attempts. Prices, totals and points are calculated on our server, not on your phone. Each order can be opened only with a secret that stays on the phone that placed it. No system is perfectly secure, and if we find a breach that affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

fineclub is for general ordering at cafes and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 for rewards or accounts. If you think we have, write to us and we will delete it.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers, which may keep it on servers inside or outside India. We choose providers that protect data to a high standard.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for cafes, tell you before it applies.

Grievance contact

For any complaint or request about your data, write to support@fineclub.in. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 7 days.

Who these terms are for

These terms apply to cafes, restaurants and hotels that use fineclub ("cafe", "you"), and to guests who order through a fineclub QR ("guest"). By using fineclub you agree to them.

What fineclub is

fineclub is software. It shows your menu, takes orders, shows them in your kitchen, makes bills and runs rewards. The cafe, not fineclub, prepares the food, sets prices, serves guests and is responsible for food quality and safety. fineclub is not a payment company and does not hold your sales money.

Fees for cafes

  • Setup fee: ₹649, paid once, for setting up your menu, table QR codes and printer. Setup work starts when you pay, so the setup fee is not refundable after that.
  • Per-order fee: ₹7 for each order a guest places through your QR. It is shown to the guest as a convenience fee on their bill. Counter orders you enter yourself are included.
  • No monthly subscription. You can stop at any time.
  • Fees are shown without taxes. If taxes apply to our fees, they are added to the invoice.
  • We may change fees with at least 30 days notice. A change never applies to orders already placed.

Weekly settlement

The per-order fees collected through your QR are settled with fineclub every week by UPI. Your dashboard shows what is due. If a week is not settled within the grace period shown on your dashboard (7 days unless we agree otherwise), we may pause your QR menu until it is settled. Guests then see that the cafe is temporarily unavailable. The QR starts again as soon as the dues are cleared.

Payments from guests

Guests pay the cafe directly through UPI or at the counter. An order counts as paid only after the cafe confirms it. Refunds, wrong orders and complaints about food are between the guest and the cafe.

What cafes must do

  • Keep your menu, prices, taxes and photos correct, and only upload content you have the right to use.
  • Follow food safety, FSSAI, GST and other laws that apply to your business. Choose your GST rate truthfully. If you are not registered for GST, set it to 0%.
  • Keep your PIN private. You are responsible for activity under your login.
  • Use guest data only to serve orders and for fair offers, and respect requests to stop messages.
  • Do not place fake orders or misuse rewards.

Guests, rewards and games

Points are given by the cafe for confirmed orders, an approved Google review, or a game played honestly. Points and vouchers have no cash value, may expire, and each cafe may change or end its rewards. Scores are checked on our server. Using bots, edited requests or other tricks can lead to points being cancelled. Rewards are available only at the cafe that issued them.

Acceptable use

Do not break the law, attack or overload the service, try to see other people's orders or data, copy the service, or use it to send spam. We may suspend accounts that do.

Service availability

We work to keep fineclub running but cannot promise it will never be down. Online ordering needs internet. Keep a way to take orders manually.

Our content and your content

fineclub, its design and software belong to us. Your menu, photos and data belong to you, and you allow us to use them to run the service for you.

Ending the service

You can stop using fineclub at any time. We may suspend or end a cafe account for unpaid dues, breaking these terms or misuse, with notice where possible. You can ask for your data to be deleted after you leave.

Limits of our responsibility

To the extent the law allows, fineclub is not responsible for loss caused by a cafe, a payment or UPI app, a printer, or an internet or power failure. Our total responsibility to a cafe for any claim is limited to the fees that cafe paid us in the three months before the claim.

Law and disputes

These terms follow the laws of India. We prefer to settle disputes by talking first. If that fails, the courts of India where our registered office is located will decide.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date above. Cafes will be told about important changes before they apply. Contact: support@fineclub.in.
